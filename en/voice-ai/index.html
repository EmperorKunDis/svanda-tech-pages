Voice AI and telephone assistants

A voice product is an end-to-end loop: audio capture, recognition, decision, integration, and a clear response at acceptable latency.

A connected pipeline

Noisy audio affects transcription, which shapes intent and the next action. A robust system handles silence, interruption, recognition errors, and user corrections.

Latency and verification

Measure time to first response and completion across real devices, voices, noisy environments, and interruptions. Actions that affect customers or records can require a repeat-back and explicit confirmation.

Examples and limits

M.I.K.E. is a public Mac alpha, not evidence that every production voice response has been qualified. Záznamník.pro's audited source differs from the deployed revision, and its SLA and every notification channel are not established by the audit.

Common questions

How should an AI project start?

Start with a specific process, its inputs and outputs, data owner, and the person who approves a result. Only then choose a model or automation tool.

Does the solution need an AI agent?

No. Stable, known rules are often better served by conventional software or a fixed workflow. Use a model where language, documents, or variable inputs matter.

How do you verify results?

Choose representative tasks and edge cases in advance. Depending on the risk, evaluate correctness, source traceability, failure behavior, permissions, and human approval.
