Martin Švanda · systems architect

Systems make sense in context.

I am Martin Švanda, a software developer and architect. I look at how people’s work, data and tools connect, then design and build systems that fit those relationships and make everyday work easier.

What I work on

My work covers agentic workflows, retrieval over company knowledge, local language models, voice interfaces, and backend integrations. I choose the simplest approach that meets the process, privacy, quality, and operating requirements.
