About me

Martin Švanda

I am Martin Švanda. I work on software from workflow design through implementation. Today I connect that experience with agent systems and local AI.

From websites and games to business software and AI

My work spans custom web applications, my own game projects, and backend development in e-commerce and industry. My experience at Speed4Trade and Liebherr, together with product development, informs the AI systems I build at Praut today. I continue to write code and connect the parts of each system myself.

The model is one component. My work is the system around it.

One workflow, different roles

For an accounting workspace, I designed a shared view of clients, tasks, documents and approvals. The interface distinguishes owner, employee and client perspectives. The result is an interactive frontend prototype using local data.

A decision you can see in the design

Hiding a button for a role is different from checking permission on a server. In this prototype I keep screen and workflow design separate from production authentication and the backend. I apply the same distinction to agents: a proposed step, permission to perform it, and a verified outcome are three different things.
