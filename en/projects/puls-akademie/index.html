Engineering decisions and tradeoffs

Human assessment

Lecturer judgment has an identifiable author and traceable feedback.

Tradeoff: Assessment capacity depends on lecturers; the platform automates records and follow-up work.

Revisions and transactions

Concurrent reviews must not change already decided work.

Tradeoff: The frontend must display conflicts and revision-request state correctly.

Tasks after commit

Workers receive identifiers of actually saved decisions.

Tradeoff: Progress, XP and communication can briefly lag behind the main request.

These decisions are editorial readings of the cited source, not documented formal ADRs.
