Problem, role and reviewed outcome

Make a browser game accessible without blindly accepting client results.

Role described in the project study: Gameplay, graphics and server-side score protection

Outcome at the cited review: A game described as live at the audit with three weapons and three robot types; targeted tests and workflows passed at the reviewed revision. Anti-cheat is heuristic and there is no playable fallback without WebGL2.

What runs in the browser and what the server checks.

A token prevents repeated submission of a session, not falsification of the entire client simulation.

Known evidence limits

  • Anti-cheat is heuristic because simulation and submitted statistics originate on the client.
  • Rate limiting is process-local and the live MySQL path lacks an actual end-to-end test.
  • No playable fallback exists without WebGL2; performance varies on weaker mobile GPUs.
