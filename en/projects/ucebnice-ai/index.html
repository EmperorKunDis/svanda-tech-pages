Engineering decisions and tradeoffs

Canonical content before tutor output

The tutor receives chapter content and explicitly untrusted student data.

Tradeoff: Prompt boundaries do not guarantee correct answers or replace approval rules.

Idempotency and learning-state revisions

Submissions, drafts and rewards must tolerate retries and concurrency without double writes.

Tradeoff: The client must send request keys and handle stale-revision conflicts.

These decisions are editorial readings of the cited source, not documented formal ADRs.
