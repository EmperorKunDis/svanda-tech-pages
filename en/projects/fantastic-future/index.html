Engineering decisions and tradeoffs

Separate script and rendering

Text preparation and voice synthesis have different requirements and retry reasons.

Tradeoff: Episode structure must stay consistent across both stages.

Segment-level cache

Repeated rendering can reuse valid sections.

Tradeoff: Renderer and voice-reference changes require correct cache versioning.

Measure actual duration

Word count is an estimate; the final report measures audio.

Tradeoff: The script may need expansion. Text validation alone cannot guarantee an hour-long episode.

These decisions are editorial readings of the cited source, not documented formal ADRs.
