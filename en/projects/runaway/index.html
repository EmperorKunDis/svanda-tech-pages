Engineering decisions and tradeoffs

Uncomplicated controls

Movement and jumping keep entry straightforward.

Tradeoff: Simplicity places more weight on movement response and obstacle placement.

Segment composition

Prepared sections provide a repeatable way to build levels.

Tradeoff: Random choice does not guarantee balanced difficulty or flawless connections.

Playable prototype as milestone

The prealpha demonstrates the core loop and visual direction.

Tradeoff: The public version lacks a menu and full UI and is not presented as a finished commercial game.

These decisions are editorial readings of the cited source, not documented formal ADRs.
